About PK Mart

Pakistan's growing online shopping destination — bringing quality products to your doorstep at prices that make sense.

infoWho We Are

PK Mart is an online shopping store built for Pakistan. We connect everyday shoppers across the country with a wide range of quality products — from everyday essentials to trending finds — all in one simple, secure marketplace. Whether you're shopping from Rawalpindi, Lahore, Karachi, or a small town in between, PK Mart brings the store to you.

We started PK Mart with a simple belief: online shopping in Pakistan should be easy, affordable, and trustworthy. Every feature on our platform, from browsing to checkout to delivery, is built around that promise.
