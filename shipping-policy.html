publicDelivery Coverage

PK Mart delivers to customers across Pakistan, including major cities and most smaller towns, through our network of courier partners.

inventoryOrder Processing Time

Orders are typically processed and prepared for dispatch within 1–2 business days of confirmation. Processing time does not include weekends or public holidays.

delivery_diningEstimated Delivery Time

Once dispatched, orders typically arrive within 3–7 business days, depending on your location. Customers in major cities generally receive orders sooner than those in more remote areas.

track_changesOrder Tracking

Where available, you will receive tracking information for your order once it has been dispatched, so you can follow its progress until it reaches you.

event_busyDelivery Delays

Occasionally, delivery may take longer than expected due to public holidays, extreme weather, or delays with our courier partners. We appreciate your patience in these situations and will keep you informed of any significant delay affecting your order.

helpQuestions About Your Delivery?

If your order is taking longer than expected or you have questions about shipping, our support team is here to help.
